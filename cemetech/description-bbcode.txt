[b]Drift Boss CE — one button, endless corners.[/b]

Hold 2nd or Enter to drift right; release to drift left. Time each switch early and keep your momentum under control as the road twists, narrows, and climbs.

This unofficial native adaptation brings Drift Boss to the TI-84 Plus CE with eight unlockable vehicles, five track themes, ten track-pattern generators in both directions, ramps and split roads, coins, a garage, and three boosters: Double Score, Car Insurance, and Coin Rush. Best score, cars, coins, and booster stock are saved when you quit from the garage.

Version 0.2 improves the fixed-point simulation and cached native graphics. The owner reports about 22 FPS on a TI-84 Plus CE Python running OS 5.8.3 through CEaShell; performance varies.

Includes the native C source, generated artwork banks, all required runtime libraries, install/build instructions, screenshots, and GIFs. A single-transfer .b84 bundle is also included. Requires an existing working C/ASM launch setup. Approximately 214 KiB archive and at least 60 KiB free user RAM. No OS update or ROM is included.

Original game and artwork: MarketJS. Native adaptation/release: Lord-Funion. This is a calculator adaptation with simplified physics and rendering, not the original browser executable. Media is captured from the native renderer with scripted inputs, not recorded on physical hardware.
